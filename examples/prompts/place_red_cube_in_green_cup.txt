Task: Place the red cube inside the green cup.

Observe the tabletop and robot state using both available exterior cameras. This rig has no wrist camera.

Your objective is to pick up the small red cube and place it completely inside the upright green cup.

Success conditions:

1. The red cube is inside the green cup.
2. The cup remains upright and has not been knocked over.
3. The gripper has released the cube and is completely outside the cup.
4. The gripper is closed after withdrawing from the cup.

Safety and execution requirements:

- Use small, controlled movements.
- Avoid contact with the cup rim, table, camera equipment, and any cables.
- Keep the cup upright throughout the task.
- Observe and verify the result after each meaningful movement.
- Never assume that a grasp or placement succeeded without checking the observations.
- If the scene is uncertain, an obstacle blocks the path, or safe execution cannot be established, stop and request human assistance.
- Do not attempt unrelated tasks.

Stop after the single task is completed and report whether all success conditions are satisfied.

If the gripper or required clearance cannot be verified from these views,
stop and explain exactly what needs to be made visible.
